Create a New Playlist

Step 1: Select Playlist Category

1. Open the DSMediaplay control panel.

2. In the dropdown menu "Select category," choose "Playlists".

3. In the dropdown menu "Select action," choose "CSV - Playlist Settings".

Step 2: Enter Playlist Data

1. Click the "Input Command" button.

2. Copy the column of new playlist names from your table and paste them into the displayed field.

3. Click "Save" to confirm the playlist names.

Step 3: Run the Action

1. Return to the main menu of the DSMediaplay control panel.

2. Click the "Start" button to create the playlists.
