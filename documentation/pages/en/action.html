Creating a New Playlist

Step 1: Selecting the Playlist Category

1. Open the DSMediaplay control panel.

2. In the "Select Category" dropdown menu, choose "Playlists."

3. In the "Select Action" dropdown menu, choose "CSV - Playlist Settings."

Step 2: Inputting the Playlist Data

1. Click the "Input Prompt" button.

2. Copy the column of new playlist names from your spreadsheet and paste it into the input box that appears.

3. Click "Save" to confirm the playlist names.

Step 3: Running the Action

1. Return to the main menu of the DSMediaplay control panel.

2. Click the "Start" button to create the playlists.
